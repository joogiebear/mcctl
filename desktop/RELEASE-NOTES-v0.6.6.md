Controls with depth

A polish pass on every form control in the panel, bringing them into the same
visual language as the surfaces around them.

- **Fields are carved, not painted.** Text boxes and dropdowns now carry a dark
  inset along their top edge — the exact opposite of the light edge raised
  cards catch — which is what makes a field read as a place to put something.
- **Dropdowns get the interface's own chevron**, ink at rest and lapis when
  focused, instead of the operating system's grey arrow; their option lists
  stop being white on a dark page.
- **Radio choices are drawn**, with the lapis dot growing in as the choice
  lands. **Switches** became troughs their knobs actually sit in.
- **Number fields lose the spinner buttons** — ports and counts are typed, not
  clicked up from zero — and the search-clear × and time-picker glyph are
  redrawn to belong to the page.
- **Segmented pickers** (console filters, chart ranges) sit in the same carved
  trough the fields do, and plain buttons pick up the subtle top-lit gradient
  the primary button already had.
- The **first-run wizard's** fields match, so the first screen and the second
  agree.
